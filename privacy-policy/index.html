Privacy Policy

How Happy Homes Interiors collects, respects, and protects your information.

Effective Date: January 1, 2024

Happy Homes Interiors ("we", "us", or "our"), operating in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, values your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website (https://happyhomesinteriors.com) and consultation channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a consultation request, request a quote, or contact us through WhatsApp or phone, we may collect the following details:

  • Full Name
  • Contact Information (Mobile phone number, WhatsApp number, Email address)
  • Property Information (Location in Coimbatore or Tamil Nadu, property type such as apartment, villa, or renovation)
  • Project Scope, requirements, budget preferences, and move-in timeline

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for legitimate business purposes related to your interior design inquiry:

  • To schedule in-person studio consultations or site measurement visits
  • To prepare customized 2D/3D floorplans and itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) estimates
  • To communicate project milestones, updates, and design drafts via phone, email, or WhatsApp
  • To provide post-handover warranty and maintenance support

3. Data Protection & Zero Third-Party Selling

We do not sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing brokers or advertising companies under any circumstances. Your information is only accessible by authorized Happy Homes Interiors project managers and design architects directly involved in your project.

4. Communication Preferences

You may request to update, modify, or delete your contact information from our records at any time by contacting us at happyhomesinteriorscbe@gmail.com or messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 999 431 1447.
