Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our interior design workflow, materials, pricing, and project guarantees in Coimbatore.

Design Process & Planning

Our process begins with an in-depth consultation at our Coimbatore studio or at your site, where we discuss your lifestyle, aesthetic preferences, and budget. Our designers take laser measurements and develop photorealistic 3D renders with material samples. Once designs and BOQ are finalized, fabrication begins at our factory followed by dust-free on-site installation.

Yes. Every client receives comprehensive 3D photorealistic renderings and 2D CAD elevation drawings. This ensures you visualize colors, lighting, textures, and storage configurations with complete clarity before fabrication commences.

Absolutely. Everything we manufacture is bespoke. You can choose from over 200+ laminate shades, acrylics, natural veneers, lacquered glass, and ceramic finishes to match your exact home theme.

Materials & Durability

For all wet and high-usage zones (kitchens, bathrooms, utility areas), we strictly use IS 710 Boiling Waterproof (BWP) Marine Grade Plywood and high-density HDHMR boards. For dry bedroom wardrobes and TV units, we use calibrated commercial ply with anti-termite treatment.

We partner with leading global hardware manufacturers including Blum (Austria), Hettich (Germany), and Hafele (Germany). All hinges and drawer runners come with soft-closing dampeners tested for 100,000 opening cycles.

Yes. All our raw boards undergo vacuum pressure impregnation with anti-termite and anti-borer chemicals. We provide a 10-year warranty against manufacturing defects and termite degradation.

Timelines & Execution

A standalone modular kitchen or wardrobe suite is typically installed within 3 to 4 weeks. Complete turnkey 2BHK or 3BHK home interior projects typically require 6 to 8 weeks depending on the civil and ceiling scope.

No. Because 90% of cutting, edge-banding, and drilling is completed in our computerized factory, on-site work is primarily modular assembly and fitting. This ensures a clean, fast, and dust-free on-site experience.

Yes. For turnkey projects, our in-house team manages civil demolition, plumbing modifications, electrical rewiring, tile laying, false ceiling framing, and painting seamlessly under one roof.

Pricing & Warranty

Costs are calculated transparently based on square footage, selected core material (BWP Ply / HDHMR), shutter finish (Acrylic, PU, Laminate, Veneer), and hardware accessories. We provide a detailed Itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with fixed prices and zero hidden costs.

We back our craftsmanship with a comprehensive 10-year warranty on all modular cabinetry structures, alongside 1 year of free on-site service support for hinges, drawer alignments, and hardware adjustments.

While our central studio is located in Coimbatore, we actively undertake turnkey residential projects across Tirupur, Erode, Pollachi, Ooty, and throughout Tamil Nadu.
