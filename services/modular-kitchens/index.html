Modular Kitchens

Ergonomic, modern modular kitchens crafted with German hardware, premium waterproof carcasses, and scratch-resistant acrylic or PU finishes.

Service Overview

Architectural Precision for Your Modular Kitchens

At Happy Homes Interiors, we design modular kitchens that are the harmonious heart of your home. Engineered around the golden work triangle (Sink, Cooktop, and Refrigerator), our modular kitchens blend ergonomic storage efficiency with timeless aesthetic appeal. Every kitchen is factory-crafted with precision-milled joinery to resist moisture, tropical heat, and heavy daily usage.

Design Possibilities for Modular Kitchens

Explore customized layout possibilities engineered to suit your space, habits, and architectural style.

Island & Breakfast Counter Kitchens

Execution Process

Our 4-Step Modular Kitchens Workflow

From initial laser measurements to dust-free on-site installation and 10-year warranty handover.

01

Site Measurement & Cooking Habit Consultation

Detailed millimeter-level laser survey and understanding of your cooking workflow, spice storage, and utility layout.

02

Photorealistic 3D Visuals & Material Curation

Interactive 3D renders with color selections, lighting accents, and finish samples examined side-by-side.

03

Precision Factory Fabrication

Computerized edge-banding and machine-drilled hardware mortises ensuring 100% dust-free on-site assembly.

04

Clean Installation & 10-Year Warranty Handover

Rapid 4-to-6 day installation by master carpenters followed by thorough testing of all drawer mechanisms.
